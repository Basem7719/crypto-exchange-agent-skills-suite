# Coin Analysis Prompt Template

Use to ask for a one-coin focused analysis.

---

> **Prompt:**
>
> Analyze {{COIN}} on {{EXCHANGE}} ({{MARKET_TYPE}}) at {{TIMEFRAME}}.
> Capital: {{CAPITAL}} USDT.
> Risk: {{RISK_PCT}}%.
>
> Include:
> - snapshot
> - technical view
> - {{INCLUDE_FUTURES?}} futures view (funding/OI/L/S/liq)
> - liquidity
> - entry / stop / TP plan
> - composite score and verdict

Replace placeholders and paste to your agent.
